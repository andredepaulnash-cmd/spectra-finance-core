# Spectra Finance Alpha 2.0 — Execution Provenance and Limitations

## Execution modes

Spectra Finance Alpha 2.0 supports **simulated paper execution only**.

- `kraken_paper_trade` is Hummingbot's simulated `PaperTradeExchange` wrapper.
- It is backed by Kraken **public** market data and a separate simulated account.
- It is **not** a real Kraken exchange sandbox, live exchange, or independently verifiable exchange environment.
- `kraken` is used only for public candle/ticker data. It is never used for order submission.

## Record classification

Current Alpha 2.0 records are classified as follows:

| Record | Classification |
|---|---|
| `FILL_SETTLED` events | Internally settled accounting records |
| `OPEN` / `RESERVED` positions | Internal ledger positions |
| `kraken_paper_trade` order history | Connector-simulated order records |
| `kraken_paper_trade` trade history | Connector-simulated trade records |
| `buy://...` order/trade identifiers | Generated by `PaperTradeExchange`, not exchange-issued IDs |
| HYPE partial fill | Unverified connector-simulated record; quarantined, not reconciled |
| PORTAL purchase | Evidence-matched connector-simulated fill; not independently exchange-confirmed |

No record in Alpha 2.0 is currently independently exchange-confirmed.

## What would be required for an independently verified purchase

1. A real exchange sandbox or live exchange environment with independently queryable order and trade history.
2. A connector whose order and trade identifiers originate from that exchange.
3. Matching order ID, trading pair, side, quantity, price, gross amount, and fee across dispatch, connector history, and trade history.
4. Governor settlement only after all evidence matches.
5. Preservation of the reservation until settlement is proven.

## Known limitations

- No live trading mode exists.
- VCAP and ordinary DCA are disabled.
- `TOP_LOSER_REVERSAL` and adaptive DTTP/DTSL are not enabled.
- The paper connector is simulated, not a real exchange sandbox.
- Existing HYPE order remains unresolved and quarantined.
- Internal accounting records are not proof of exchange execution.

## Product identity

Spectra Finance is the product. Hummingbot and Condor are underlying implementation foundations. Alpha 2.0 is built on Hummingbot (connectors, order lifecycle, API) and Condor (research, scheduling, orchestration), but the product identity remains Spectra Finance.
