# Historical PARK ownership and data audit

This package describes saved historical evidence. It does not run a new backtest, hedge simulation or parameter search. Production `spectra-core-phase2` remains untouched. The October 9–November 9 holdout outcomes and nested sealed archive were not opened or scored; only the enclosing historical package was hashed as opaque bytes.

The validated baseline archive SHA256 is `440080615478a7d4974f347f6e9efda7efceb557adc20d92db75677f8f88459d`. PR #26 is pinned at `931d296775425fbea26140ecf2a654767b691874`. Git primary files match their exact blob identities, selected historical archive members match their SHA256 manifest, and the historical capital-quality package matches its Git-pinned receipt. Detailed source bindings and reconstruction steps are in `Data_Provenance_Verification.json`.

The saved January–June prefix starts with $2,000 cash and zero owned positions. It reconciles 1,630 modeled fills, 224 acquired assets and 37 ending assets to the exact June checkpoint. The saved ending equity is $1,898.27718309, with $202.02714697 realized P&L, $303.74996388 open depreciation and $61.74908694 fees. Costs already embedded in the model are counted once. These are simulator records, not venue-confirmed fills or current wallet balances.

The June-to-July continuation maps all 57 genuine PARK terminal lots through immutable buy order identities. Thirty-seven starting assets become 36 ending assets: one starting asset increases units, three decrease and 33 remain unchanged; two new assets remain held. All 29 modeled fills, asset quantities, fee-inclusive basis, cash and fees reconcile against the unchanged saved PARK control. Dollar tolerance is $1e-18; base-unit residuals and native rounding residuals remain exported. Units from different assets are never added into a portfolio gain.

For the July boundary, exact PARK units are multiplied by same-asset historical market marks in a SHA-verified prior independent tape receipt, rather than substituting another arm's inventory. All shared mark identities agree, and the total matches PARK marked inventory. Market-value HHI is 0.03773510, equivalent to 26.50 equal-sized holdings; the largest five account for 29.23% of inventory value. Eighteen assets have marks older than five minutes, totaling $91.74883424. HHI is an inventory weight measure, not proof of correlation, hedge quality or liquidity.

All residuals remain owned property. No dust threshold, minimum-compliant liquidation, active-slot release, reentry finality or strategic-retention classification is proven here. Historical last closes exclude future sale fees, spread, price impact and execution uncertainty. Ordinary and parked cash remain distinct from marked holdings and realized P&L.

Data availability is recorded in `Data_Availability_And_Gaps.json`. Six monthly spot archives and the fixed619 Q3 derived spot archive are resolved by filename and size; their raw bytes were not downloaded in this audit. The canonical extended array has a recorded size of 2,336,076,416 bytes and SHA256 `dde300cb04c5f8c85aafba3924f74e70df1477ce26ca4c1597d295202d047938`, but is not present as a resolved standalone artifact. Prepared metadata includes SOL-USD and excludes BTC-USD/ETH-USD. Genuine benchmark history, point-in-time lifecycle and account eligibility, funding, borrow, margin and liquidation inputs remain gaps before a historical hedge performance run.

Current public catalog overlap is descriptive only: ALGO, BCH, HBAR and RENDER overlap the advertised online US margin short catalog; BCH and HBAR overlap the advertised US perpetual launch list. Exact current symbol matches do not prove historical instrument availability, current account approval or borrow availability. No aliases are inferred. Public sources and matched rules are retained in the overlap JSON.
