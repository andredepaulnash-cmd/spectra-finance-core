## What the completed evidence establishes

All three examples below began with $2,000, used the same 40bps fee/5bps slippage assumptions and the finite cash-owner adapter. Amounts are rounded USD.

| Audited owner cohort | Realized net P&L | Retained marked P&L | Whole-account marked P&L |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Matched owner control | +203.92 | -367.09 | -163.17 |
| Confirmed partial-principal redeployment | +380.14 | -714.11 | -333.97 |
| Supported combined hypothesis | +127.25 | -440.06 | -312.82 |

This is an accounting explanation: realized plus unrealized P&L equals total marked P&L. Costs are already accounted for in realized disposals and remaining basis; do not subtract paid fees again. Modeled terminal disposal costs reduce equity further. Selling an underwater position changes where the loss is recorded and adds execution costs; it does not erase the loss.

The redeployment comparison shows that increasing realized gains did not improve this matched simulation’s whole account. It does not prove that all redeployment, recovery, rotation or Spectra strategies fail. The six-month inspected development tape, declared source and execution surrogate define the scope. Accounting PASS establishes internal consistency, not a venue-tested profitable strategy.

The [funding diagnostics](funding-and-source-diagnostics.md) attribute the owner control’s marked P&L to BASE lots (-$166.36) and RECOVERY lots (+$3.19). This identifies where the recorded loss resides. It does **not** estimate what would have happened with different entries or with recovery disabled: either change alters later prices, cash availability, inventory and decisions. Those require matched counterfactual runs.

## Daily productivity and finite capital

The strategy-free daily oracle records owner-control confirmed sales on 55 of 181 UTC days. On 24 days realized profit increased while whole-account equity fell. Occupied-slot time was 99.97% of fifty-slot capacity. Full slots therefore did not mean daily monetization or account growth.

The next coin-selection hypothesis should judge **repeated, executable opportunities after costs**, using only evidence available before admission. Predeclare measures for opportunity frequency, directional/volatility behavior, stale or missing observations, quote capacity, turnover cost and retained-loss exposure. A coin’s eventual six-month profit, future listing survival, or one exceptional price spike cannot choose its earlier membership. List refresh should govern future admissions; treatment of already-held positions needs its own declared inventory policy.

The original Architect per-coin ladder map is still unrecovered. The causal ladder hypothesis assigned UNKNOWN to 365 of 366 parents. That is incomplete profile evidence, not a faithful test of the original unique coin ladders. Preserve that distinction.

Capital remains physically finite and owned. PRODUCTIVE cash cannot silently finance VCAP, and two owner admission maxima share one physical reserve ceiling. In the completed owner control, productive cash could fund the fixed entry ticket throughout, while VCAP could afford its minimum modeled ticket in only 0.4393% of samples. The ladder arm retained VCAP affordability but was frequently blocked by UNKNOWN profiles. Funding, profile evidence, slots, pair limits and execution are separate constraints. Releasing a settled partial receipt cannot create principal or release another receipt’s hold. Ordinary whole-sale proceeds already return deployable cash to their original owners; PARK in this cohort applies to partial recovery proceeds.

## What the next experiment would resolve

Use a matched design that isolates two questions:

| Question | Predeclared comparison | Decision evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Does causal daily coin admission improve entries? | Identical owner/cost/exit/recovery rules, with one frozen admission hypothesis changed | Whole-account net return, negative-tail inventory, daily equity changes and profit per capital-dollar-day |
| Does a bounded retained-inventory policy improve capital use? | Identical admission rules, with one explicit inventory policy changed | Net effect of realized loss, disposal costs, released same-owner capital and later opportunities |

A combined cell is useful only if declared in advance to measure the interaction. Keep the original BASE entry and lineage evidence, protect armed native profit floors, and distinguish whole-parent retirement from parcel recycling. Do not choose retirement times or coin thresholds from the eventual losing positions. Any loss-taking rule would need explicit new protocol semantics; this memo does not introduce one.

Before execution, freeze source hashes, hypotheses and exact formulas, costs, eligible universe, owner budgets, capital sizes, pair minima, missing-data rules, warmup, cash/reserve accounting, arbitration priorities, drawdown/termination criteria and the complete trial budget. Preserve matched controls. Freeze the complete set before outcomes; do not improve thresholds between arms or periods. Already inspected tape can support development comparisons but cannot become fresh proof by being rerun.
