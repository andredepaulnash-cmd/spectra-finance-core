# Continuous profitability campaign

Architect identified three missing economic ingredients: a list of coins with frequent usable moves, coin-specific funded recovery ladders, and keeping up to 50 distinct trading positions productive through appropriate replacement. This campaign tests those ideas causally against the preserved native January–June five-minute research bridge.

Both original controls reproduced exactly and passed independent audits of all 104,256 five-minute samples and every serialized fill. They ended at $1,898.28 and $1,941.81 from $2,000. Fourteen completed runs now pass independent accounting audit, and all have negative whole-account profit. CI verifies fictional causal and accounting fixtures; a passing CI run does not prove financial profitability. Exact finalized results and terminal valuation sensitivities are retained in `completed-development-results.json`.

The exact-owner control kept 99.97% of its 50-slot capacity occupied after initialization, yet lost $163.17: $203.92 realized gains were outweighed by $367.09 open losses. Keeping slots full alone therefore did not make the account productive. Weekly membership refresh lost $61.63 with 6.65% drawdown; its lower drawdown does not establish profitability. Current-cost comparisons must stay within their matched owner/cost cohort because changing costs also changes the execution path.

The unchanged $2,000 control realized $202.03 through June while its remaining inventory was $303.75 underwater, leaving $1,898.28 marked equity. Its 37 terminal positions had a median age of about 175 days. Much of its $1,702.86 cash was deliberately parked; only $1.54 was deployable at that boundary. Replacing an absolute-volatility rank with recurring cost-adjusted opportunities and attributing capital ownership are therefore substantive hypotheses, not cosmetic changes.

These are screening proxies, not obtainable profit or evidence of buying the trough. The native account replay must show which opportunities were actually captured.

## Accounting and module coverage

The original bridge uses fungible shared cash with a physical reserve, rather than complete separate productive and VCAP balances. Original controls preserve that behavior and PARK proceeds. The new exact receipt-driven owner adapter and both matched cost-cohort controls have now passed independent full-path audits.

The new owner model starts with $1,500 productive cash and $500 VCAP cash. Principal returns to the exact disposed lot's original owner. Positive realized profit from each disposed lot is allocated 75% productive and 25% VCAP; a negative lot result stays with its original owner and remains visible in account P&L. Fees remain embedded in exact basis and are charged once. Pending orders keep their owner locks until confirmed settlement or confirmed cancellation. The physical reserve, pair limits and economic base identity remain additional constraints.

In this owner cohort, PARK applies to confirmed partial RECOVERY sale receipts. Confirmed ordinary whole-position proceeds are already deployable under their original owners. Explicit partial redeployment releases only its own settled receipt, preserving unrelated holds. Per-owner admission maxima share one physical reserve ceiling and cannot be added together as independent budgets. `owner-park-scope-clarification.json` records existing source behavior without changing a frozen policy.

The 50-position rule is a funded admission target. Its shortfalls and rejection reasons must be reported. It cannot manufacture cash, duplicate a retained asset, consume protected funds or turn an unfilled exit into a free slot.

The recovery-quality gate is an independent optional mechanism controlled by each arm's `recovery_quality` flag. It is off in a ladder-only arm; classifying a coin as deep does not establish that it deserves recovery.

Included research boundaries are the native lot ledger, partial recycling, exit/recovery accounting, causal selector and bounded policy hooks. Production persistence, actual exchange reconciliation, route-specific minimums and live module execution remain separate evidence gates. A pure research arbiter is not a demonstration that every production module is fully integrated.

The arm named `combined_full_supported` contains selector, directional, recovery-quality, new ladders and confirmed partial redeployment. It does not include the separate market-regime or weekly-refresh mechanisms and is not full-product integration. The native DTTP arm exercises actual floor/giveback behavior; DTSS, DTSSR and PSSM remain unexercised.

Every finalized arm also receives hypothetical terminal exit-cost and stale-mark-zero sensitivities. These create no fills or released cash and do not prove venue liquidation. Operating costs and taxes are not included in modeled trading P&L; financial claims require genuine unseen validation after development selection.
