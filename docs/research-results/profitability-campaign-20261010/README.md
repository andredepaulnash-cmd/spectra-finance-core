This campaign tests those ideas causally against the preserved native January–June five-minute research bridge.

The first original-control reproductions are running. No new campaign profit result is established by this preparation commit. CI verifies fictional causal and accounting fixtures; a passing CI run does not prove financial profitability.

Its 37 terminal positions had a median age of about 175 days. Much of its $1,702.86 cash was deliberately parked; only $1.54 was deployable at that boundary. Replacing an absolute-volatility rank with recurring cost-adjusted opportunities and attributing capital ownership are therefore substantive hypotheses, not cosmetic changes.

## Accounting and module coverage

The original bridge uses fungible shared cash with a physical reserve, rather than complete separate productive and VCAP balances. Original controls preserve that behavior and PARK proceeds. Ownership-sensitive ladder/reinvestment arms stay gated until the new exact receipt-driven owner adapter and matched owner control pass independent audit.

The new owner model starts with $1,500 productive cash and $500 VCAP cash. Principal returns to the exact disposed lot's original owner. Positive realized profit from each disposed lot is allocated 75% productive and 25% VCAP; a negative lot result stays with its original owner and remains visible in account P&L. Fees remain embedded in exact basis and are charged once. Pending orders keep their owner locks until confirmed settlement or confirmed cancellation. The physical reserve, pair limits and economic base identity remain additional constraints.

The 50-position rule is a funded admission target. Its shortfalls and rejection reasons must be reported. It cannot manufacture cash, duplicate a retained asset, consume protected funds or turn an unfilled exit into a free slot.

The recovery-quality gate is an independent optional mechanism controlled by each arm's `recovery_quality` flag. It is off in a ladder-only arm; classifying a coin as deep does not establish that it deserves recovery.

Included research boundaries are the native lot ledger, partial recycling, exit/recovery accounting, causal selector and bounded policy hooks. Production persistence, actual exchange reconciliation, route-specific minimums and live module execution remain separate evidence gates. A pure research arbiter is not a demonstration that every production module is fully integrated.
