## Proposed continuing responsibility

Assign the same identified Dot as the Spectra Finance research controller, with six bounded work areas:

1. Admit and version market, listing, fee, minimum-size and execution evidence.
2. Maintain causal asset-list proposals emphasizing repeated after-cost opportunity and capital recovery time.
3. Evaluate coin-class DCA / VCAP ladders while preserving finite allocations and ownership.
4. Coordinate entry, recovery, rotation, protection and exit intents using explicit precedence and reservations.
5. Independently audit total equity, remaining inventory losses and failure cases before advancing a candidate.
6. Prepare reviewable software fixes and verify their source and compatibility evidence.

Each output carries input hashes, observation cutoff, policy version, reason, applicable delay and completion evidence. A proposal made after a historical observation can affect only later decisions. Record unfilled position slots when capital, valid candidates or execution gates prevent redeployment; a displayed full position count cannot override the reserve rules.

Notify on decisive profitability findings, invalidating evidence, material venue changes or a concrete missing decision/capability. Supply the frozen protocol and every attempt, VCAP and ladder rules, source pins, inspected-period registry, holdout exclusion and route-feasibility gates. Avoid assuming the Dot automatically receives all earlier conversations.

The continuing responsibility should be assigned in that Dot's actual conversation and checked in Activity. Fixed daily checks need an explicit saved schedule with scope, timing, timezone and destination. Merely connecting a service does not establish monitoring. OpenAI also distinguishes read-only proactive research from assigned actions. See [Tasks and memory](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/tasks-and-memory).

Existing user authorization governs the research. Account actions still require the actual connector and account capability, while production deployment and private trading are outside this preparation. Reviewable research changes can proceed in the candidate without inventing a new Dot or claiming autonomous work has been provisioned.
