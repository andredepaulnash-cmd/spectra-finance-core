## Research coordinator versus campaign integration

`module_coordination.py` supplies `decide_batch`, `confirm_recovery`, `unlock_confirmed_recycle` and `slot_shortfall`. Its42 authored adversarial tests cover exit priority, parent/rung replay, canonical bases, pending claims, typed owner budgets, protected locks/floors and defense reservations. This review does not rerun or read their result receipt. The reviewed legacy driver does not import that helper.

An accepted reservation is not a fill or an open position. Cancel requests and anticipated sales cannot release cash or identity. A caller-supplied retired status is not a native retirement certificate. `unlock_confirmed_recycle` preserves VCAP ownership in its research model; it does not replace native cash authority or make VCAP principal productive-entry funding.

The new controller must connect real module intents, pending state, native/model owner authorization and confirmed fill reconciliation in the same admitted replay. Concrete evidence needed:

1. Frozen adapter/protocol hashes and exact changed hook boundaries, retaining historical controls.
2. Recorded proposal/action/parent/owner, governor or explicitly named surrogate decision, and modeled receipt/lot/rung scope.
3. Owner cash and locks before/after every confirmed buy, sale, rejection and cancellation; no expected proceeds admitted as cash.
4. Conflict checks for protected profit versus patch/partial, whole versus partial, pending cancellation versus admission, and defense versus spot increases.
5. Separate source/runtime coverage and economic accounting audit. Genuine DTTP and short/hedge variants need separately admitted implementations and appropriate historical instruments.

The causal selector and50-slot target are real research hooks. Eligibility, minima, volume and protected cash can leave positions short. Cross-quote canonical admission, native durable restart and real exchange fills are not proved by a oneUSDpair-perasset tape.
